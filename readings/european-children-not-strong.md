# European children are not strong anymore – level 3

## 文章

A new study has revealed European children and adolescents are in worse physical shape today than at almost any point over the last 60 years.

Researchers from the University of Zaragoza analyzed physical fitness data from over 417,000 young people aged 6 to 16 across 20 European countries. The findings show a worrying long-term decline in key health indicators, including muscular strength, flexibility, and overall body composition. Upper and lower body strength have steadily dropped since the 1980s, while flexibility has experienced the steepest decline, and accelerated sharply after 2000. Additionally, researchers noted a continuous increase in body fat levels among youth.

Health experts warn that physical inactivity severely threatens future well-being, as over 75% of European adolescents fail to meet the WHO's recommendation of 60 minutes of daily physical activity.
