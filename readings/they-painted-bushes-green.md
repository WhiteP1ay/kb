# They Painted Bushes Green

Slovak Matica is a public, non-partisanship, cultural institution with a role of supporting Slovak self-determination.

And it is the institution which painted some of the shrubs in its park green last week.

They claim the bushes were already dried out and should be replaced anyway.

The "green painting" cost the institution 300 euros.

This used to be common practise when a high-profile visit was expected under socialist times in the last century.
