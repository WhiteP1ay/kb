# debris

## 音标

/dəˈbriː/

## 释义

**n.** 碎片，残骸，瓦砾（尤指被毁坏后散落的碎片）

## 例句

- After the earthquake, rescuers began digging through the debris in search of survivors.
- The npm install left so much debris in node_modules that I had to rm -rf and start over.（自造句）

## 记忆法

源自法语 débris，词根是古法语 debriser "打碎"。注意末尾 s 不发音。和 garbage/trash 的区别：debris 强调"打碎后散落的残片"，更有破碎感。

## 同义词

wreckage, rubble, remnants, detritus
