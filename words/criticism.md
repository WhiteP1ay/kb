# criticism

## 音标
/ˈkrɪtɪˌsɪzəm/

## 释义
n. 批评，批判——对某人或某事的负面评价（也指文艺作品的分析评论，此时偏中性）

## 例句
His code review consisted entirely of criticism with zero suggestions — classic "this sucks" energy without the "here's how to fix it."

Constructive criticism is a superpower: tell me my architecture is brittle AND show me where to add an abstraction layer.

## 记忆法
critic（批评家，来自希腊语 kritikos "能判断的"）+ ism（名词后缀，表"行为/体系"）→ 批评这一行为。同词根：critical（关键的，批判的）、criterion（评判标准）、crisis（危机——需要"判断"的时刻）。

## 同义词
critique, censure, condemnation, disapproval, feedback
