# evacuation

## 音标

/ɪˌvæk.juˈeɪ.ʃən/

## 释义

**n.** 疏散，撤离（将人员从危险地区转移）；排空，清空

## 例句

- On Monday, 17,000 residents were evacuated from the city. （文中例句，动词形式）
- The data center ordered an emergency evacuation after the cooling system failed and servers began overheating — ironic, given they were fleeing the very heat they were supposed to manage.

## 记忆法

词根：e-（向外）+ vacuus（空的）→ 拉丁语 evacuare "to empty out" → 把人"清空"出危险区域就是疏散。同根词：vacuum（真空）、vacant（空缺的）、vacate（腾出）。

## 同义词

exodus, withdrawal, removal, clearance
