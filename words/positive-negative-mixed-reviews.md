# positive / negative / mixed reviews

## 释义

- **positive reviews** — 好评
- **negative reviews** — 差评
- **mixed reviews** — 褒贬不一

## 例句

The movie got mostly positive reviews from critics. （这部电影获得了影评人的大部分好评）

Despite negative reviews, the film did well at the box office. （尽管差评如潮，票房却不错）

The new album received mixed reviews — some loved it, some hated it. （新专辑评价褒贬不一，有人爱有人恨）
