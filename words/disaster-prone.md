# disaster-prone

## 音标
/dɪˈzæstər proʊn/

## 释义
易招灾的 / 灾祸缠身的 / 倾向于陷入危机的

## 例句
Musk is a disaster-prone visionary — his compulsion to court chaos is what drives his grandest achievements and his most spectacular self-inflicted wounds.

My roommate is disaster-prone — he can turn boiling water into a kitchen fire.

## 记忆法
dis(分离) + aster(星) → 古罗马认为星位不正即灾祸；prone = 前倾 → 倾向。记：星歪了 = 灾祸体质

## 同义词
accident-prone, calamitous
