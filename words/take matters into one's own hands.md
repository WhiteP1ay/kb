# take matters into one's own hands

## 释义

亲自动手处理；自己接手解决（因为别人靠不住）

## 拆解

- **take** — 拿、接手
- **matters** — 事情、事务（复数，泛指各种待处理的问题）
- **into one's own hands** — 到自己手里

字面意思：把事情拿到自己手里 → 不等别人了，自己干。

## 例句

- When the police didn't respond, the neighbors took matters into their own hands and started a neighborhood watch.
- The API documentation was so outdated that I took matters into my own hands and rewrote it. （自造句 — 程序员日常）

## 常见搭配

- take matters into your own hands — 你自己动手
- take matters into his/her own hands — 他/她自己动手
