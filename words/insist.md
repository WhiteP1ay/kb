# insist

## 音标
/ɪnˈsɪst/

## 释义
v. 坚持，坚决要求，坚称

## 例句
He insists that he saw the ghosts.

The PM insisted on adding more features two days before the deadline. *(自造句)*

## 记忆法

### 词根
> in- (on, upon 在…上) + sist (站立) → 站在上面不动 → 坚持

### 谐音
> —

## 同义词
demand, assert, maintain
