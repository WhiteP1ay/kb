# exact

## 音标
/ɪɡˈzækt/

## 释义
adj. 精确的，准确的，丝毫不差的

## 例句
The exact time of the meeting is 3:00 PM.

The exact error message was "Cannot read property 'map' of undefined" — not just "it crashed somewhere".

## 记忆法
ex-（出来）+ act（驱动）= 把标准"推出来"逐项对齐 → 丝毫不差的。和 precise 是最接近的同义词，二者都强调分毫不差。

## 同义词
precise, accurate, correct
