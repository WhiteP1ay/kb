# implication

## 音标

/ˌɪmplɪˈkeɪʃən/

## 释义

**n.** ① 可能的影响、后果；② 暗示、暗指；③ 牵连、卷入

## 例句

- Scientists are rushing to understand the implications of Arctic warming for the planet's future.
- When the client casually mentioned "we might pivot," the implication for our 3-month roadmap was that half the features were now dead code walking.（自造句）

## 记忆法

词根 im-（进入）+ plic（折叠）+ -ation（名词后缀）→"折进去的东西"→引申为"隐含的意义 / 连锁影响"。锚点词：imply（暗示）、implicit（隐含的）、implicate（牵连）——全是同一个拉丁词根 plicare "折叠"。你可以想象一张纸折起来，里面夹着的信息就是 implication——表面看不到，但拉开来就在里面。

## 同义词

consequence, inference, ramification
