# common across

## 释义

在……范围内普遍存在、司空见惯。强调事物从"偶发"变成了某范围内的"常态"。

## 拆解

- **common**：普遍的、常见的
- **across**：遍及、横跨
- 搭配在一起表示"遍布于……、在……中很普遍"，后面接范围词（the globe / the industry / the region 等）

## 例句

- Extreme heat and wildfires are becoming common across the globe. （文中原句）
- Remote work is now common across the tech industry.
- This species was once rare but is now common across the region.

## 常见搭配

common across the globe / the world / the country / the industry / the region
