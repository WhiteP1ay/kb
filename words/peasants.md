# peasants

## 音标
/ˈpɛzənt/

## 释义
n. 农民，佃农；(贬) 底层群众，土包子，韭菜

## 例句
The peasants worked the land from dawn to dusk for a lord they never saw.

[[quantize]] the hell out of it until they hit the [[threshold]] of complaints, then give them random resets to keep the peasants happy.

## 记忆法
来自古法语 paisant（乡下人），词根 pays（乡村）。在互联网黑话里 peasant 讽刺那些不懂技术、被产品决策牵着走的普通用户——和"韭菜"异曲同工。

## 同义词
farmer, serf, commoner, pleb
