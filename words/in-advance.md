# in advance

## 释义

提前、事先

## 拆解

in（介词）+ advance（前进、预先）。advance 单独作动词是"前进、推进"，但在 in advance 这个固定搭配里整体表"提前"。相当于 beforehand。

## 例句

- Travelers cannot complete any steps in advance before arriving.
- You should book the hotel in advance.

## 常见搭配

book in advance, pay in advance, plan in advance, in advance of sth
