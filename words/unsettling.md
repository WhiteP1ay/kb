# unsettling

## 音标

/ʌnˈset.əl.ɪŋ/

## 释义

**adj.** 令人不安的，让人心里发毛的（让人觉得担忧或不对劲）

## 例句

- The city had an unsettling resemblance to the darkest days of the Covid-19 pandemic. （文中原句）
- Watching the AI generate a perfectly coherent but entirely false biography of me was deeply unsettling.

## 记忆法

un-（否定）+ settle（安定、安顿）+ -ing（形容词后缀）→ 让人无法安定的 → 令人不安的。settle 本身是"使安定下来"，比如 settle down（安定下来）。加上 un- 就是"安定不下来"，心里悬着。

## 同义词

disturbing, troubling, disquieting, worrying
