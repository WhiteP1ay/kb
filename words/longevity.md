# longevity

## 音标

/lɑːnˈdʒɛvəti/ (美)

## 释义

**n.** 长寿；持久

## 例句

- A healthy diet and regular exercise contribute to longevity.
- The longevity of this legacy codebase is impressive — it's been running for 15 years without a rewrite.（自造句）

## 记忆法

long（长）+ ev（时代/年龄，来自拉丁语 aevum）+ ity（名词后缀）→ "长时代" = 长寿。锚点词：medieval（中世纪的，medi-中间+ev时代）、long（长）。

## 同义词

lifespan, long life, durability
