# board of directors

## 音标
/bɔːrd əv dɪˈrɛktərz/

## 释义
董事会

## 例句
The React Foundation will be governed by a board of directors composed of representatives from each major contributor project.

The startup's board of directors voted unanimously to open-source the core inference engine.

## 记忆法
board(桌子 → 董事桌) + director(指挥者) → 围桌开会的那群人

## 同义词
executive board, governing board
