# locals

## 音标

/ˈloʊ.kəlz/

## 释义

**n.** 当地人，本地居民（local 的名词复数形式）

## 例句

- Bordeaux locals are now worrying about the future of their city. （文中原句）
- Before deploying, we always test with a handful of locals first — real users in the office who aren't on the dev team.

## 记忆法

local 作形容词是"当地的、本地的"，加 -s 变成名词"当地人"。类似的：natives（本地人）、civilians（平民）。都属于"用形容词词干加复数直接当名词用"的模式。

## 同义词

natives, residents, inhabitants, locals
