# despite

## 音标

/dɪˈspaɪt/

## 释义

prep. 尽管，不管（= in spite of）

## 例句

Despite the rain, the game continued. （尽管下雨，比赛还是继续了）

She got the job despite having no experience. （尽管没有经验，她还是拿到了那份工作）

## 关键点

- 后面接**名词/doing**，不能直接接句子
- ❌ Despite it was raining → ✓ Despite the rain
- 要接句子需加 the fact that：Despite the fact that it was raining
