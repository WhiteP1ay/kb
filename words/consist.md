# consist

## 音标
/kənˈsɪst/

## 释义
v. 由…组成；在于
- **consist of** — 由…组成（= be composed of / comprise）
- **consist in** — 在于，存在于

## 例句
His code review consisted entirely of criticism with zero suggestions.

The training dataset consists of 10 million high-quality instruction-response pairs. *(自造句)*

## 记忆法

### 词根
> con- (共同,一起) + sist (站立) → 站在一起 → 组成
> 
> 同词根词: assist (帮助), insist (坚持), persist (持续), resist (抵抗), exist (存在), consistent (一致的)

### 谐音
> —

## 同义词
be composed of, comprise, be made up of, contain
