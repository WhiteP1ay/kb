# overall

## 音标

/ˌoʊvərˈɔːl/ (adv.), /ˈoʊvərˌɔːl/ (adj.)

## 释义

**adj.** 整体的，总体的，全面的
**adv.** 总体上，总的来说

## 例句

- The overall cost of the project was higher than expected.
- Overall, I think the refactoring was worth it — the codebase is much cleaner now.（自造句）

## 记忆法

over（覆盖）+ all（全部）= 覆盖全部 → 整体的。字面就是"over all"，把一切都盖住的视角。

## 同义词

total, general, comprehensive, holistic
