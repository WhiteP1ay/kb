# severely

## 音标

/səˈvɪrli/

## 释义

**adv.** 严重地，剧烈地；严厉地

## 例句

- The drought severely affected crop yields this year.
- The production server was severely degraded after the memory leak went unnoticed for hours.（自造句）

## 记忆法

severe（严重的）+ -ly（副词后缀）。severe 本身来自拉丁语 severus（严肃、严苛），联想一个 severe bug 让整个系统 severely 受影响。

## 同义词

seriously, gravely, critically, drastically
