# re-entry

## 音标

/riːˈɛntri/

## 释义

**n.** 再入（大气层）；重新进入

## 例句

- The space station would burn up on re-entry into the Earth's atmosphere.
- After the system crash, the re-entry of data from the backup took nearly two hours.（自造句）

## 记忆法

re- "再次" + entry "进入"。航天语境中专指航天器从太空返回大气层的过程。也用于日常：re-entry into the job market（重新进入就业市场）。不加连字符写成 reentry 也可以。

## 同义词

return, re-entrance, atmospheric entry
