# the hell out of

## 音标
/ðə hɛl aʊt ʌv/

## 释义
（口语）用在动词后表强调——往死里…、拼命地…、极致地…。固定模板：verb + the hell out of + object

## 例句
Quantize [[the-hell-out-of|the hell out of]] it until they hit the [[threshold]] of complaints.

I debugged the hell out of that code before I finally found the race condition.

That horror movie scared the hell out of me.

## 记忆法
hell（地狱）→ 把某物从地狱里拽出来 → 做到极致、往死里搞。中文类比："往死里打"——不是真打死，纯粹强调程度。可替换的语气词：the crap / the shit / the fuck，越往右越粗鲁但意思不变。

## 同义词
intensely, like crazy, to the extreme, to death, to the max
