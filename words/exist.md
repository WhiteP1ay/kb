# exist

## 音标
/ɪɡˈzɪst/

## 释义
v. 存在；生存

## 例句
The world exists, whether you like it or not.

Does a bug really exist if no one reported it? *(自造句)*

## 记忆法

### 词根
> ex- (out, forth 向外) + (s)ist (站立) → 站出来 → 存在
