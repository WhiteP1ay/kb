# federation

## 音标

/ˌfed.əˈreɪ.ʃən/

## 释义

**n.** 联合会，联盟；联邦（由多个独立团体联合组成的组织）

## 例句

- France's National Firefighters Federation said the kind of wildfire outside of Bordeaux has never been seen in France before. （文中原句）
- The Kubernetes Federation (KubeFed) lets you manage multiple clusters as if they were one — a federation of independent nodes.

## 记忆法

拉丁词根 foedus（条约、盟约）→ foederare（通过条约联合）→ federation（联合体）。核心画面：多个独立个体通过"约定"绑在一起。同根词：federal（联邦的）、confederation（邦联——比 federation 更松散）。

## 同义词

union, alliance, confederation, association, league
