# precise

## 音标
/prɪˈsaɪs/

## 释义
adj. 精确的，准确的，确切的

## 例句
The GPS gave us the precise coordinates of the building.
[[coordinate]]

When debugging, you need to be precise about which line caused the crash — "somewhere in this file" doesn't cut it.

## 记忆法
pre-（预先）+ cise（切，来自拉丁语 caedere）= 事先切好的，分毫不差的 → 精确的。同词根词：decide（切断犹豫→决定）、concise（切掉多余→简洁的）、incisive（切中要害→敏锐的）。

## 同义词
exact, accurate, correct, rigorous
