# versus

## 音标

/ˈvɜːr.səs/（缩写 vs. 或 v.）

## 释义

**prep.** 对，对抗，与……相比

## 例句

- He said his firefighters were in "a David-versus-Goliath scenario". （文中原句）
- The eternal debate: tabs versus spaces — every dev has a strong opinion and nobody ever changes their mind.

## 记忆法

拉丁语 versus "转向、朝向"，原意是"转向面对某人"→ 后来变成"与某人对抗"。法律文件里用 v. 缩写的源头（如 Roe v. Wade）。口语里 vs. 更多用来做对比，不一定是对抗（iOS vs. Android）。

## 同义词

against, as opposed to, compared with
