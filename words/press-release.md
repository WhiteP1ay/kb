# press release

## 音标
/prɛs rɪˈliːs/

## 释义
新闻稿 / 新闻发布

## 例句
You can read more in the Linux Foundation's press release.

We drafted a press release to announce the launch of our new open-source AI toolkit.

## 记忆法
press(新闻界，源自印刷机) + release(释放) → 向媒体放出的消息

## 同义词
news release, statement, announcement
