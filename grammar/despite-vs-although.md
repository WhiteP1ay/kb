# despite vs although

## 核心区别

**despite** 是介词，后面接名词/doing。
**although** 是连词，后面接完整句子。

> despite 后面是"一个东西"，although 后面是"一件事"。

## 对照

| | despite | although |
|---|---|---|
| 词性 | 介词 | 连词 |
| 后面接 | 名词 / 名词短语 / doing | 完整句子（主+谓） |
| ❌ | Despite he was tired | — |
| ✓ | Despite being tired | Although he was tired |

## 例句

Despite the negative reviews, the film did well at the box office.
Although the reviews were negative, the film did well at the box office.

Despite having no experience, she got the job.
Although she had no experience, she got the job.

## 关键点

- **despite** = **in spite of**，完全同义，可以互换
- despite 后面不能直接跟 that 从句（要加 the fact that：despite the fact that...）
- although 可以和 even though 互换，though 可以放句中/句末
