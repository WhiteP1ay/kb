## disaster-prone

- **meaning**: 易招灾的 / 灾祸缠身的 / 倾向于陷入危机的
- **source**: Walter Isaacson《Elon Musk》
- **sentence**: Musk is a disaster-prone visionary — his compulsion to court chaos is what drives his grandest achievements and his most spectacular self-inflicted wounds.
- **my sentence**: My roommate is disaster-prone — he can turn boiling water into a kitchen fire.
- **create**: 2026-06-24
- **mnemonic**: dis(分离)+aster(星)→古罗马认为星位不正即灾祸；prone=前倾→倾向。记：星歪了=灾祸体质
