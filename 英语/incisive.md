# incisive

## 音标
/ɪnˈsaɪ.sɪv/

## 释义
adj. 尖锐的，深刻的，一针见血的

## 例句
She asked some incisive questions that exposed the flaws in the plan.

An incisive code reviewer doesn't just spot the bug — they identify the wrong assumption behind it.

## 记忆法
in-（进入）+ cis（切）+ ive（形容词后缀）= 切进去的 → 一针见血的。和 [[precise]] [[concise]] 同词根 cis/cise！

## 同义词
penetrating, sharp, keen, acute
