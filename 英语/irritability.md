# irritability

## 音标
/ˌɪrɪtəˈbɪlɪti/

## 释义
n. 易怒，烦躁——一种容易被打扰、被激怒的状态（医学上也指器官对刺激的过度反应）

## 例句
Three all-nighters in a row left him with the irritability of a teething toddler — even Slack notifications set him off.

The PM's constant scope changes were the main source of the team's collective irritability.

## 记忆法
irritate（激怒，刺激）+ ability（能力/性质）→ 被刺激的"能力"太强 → 易怒，烦躁。想象皮肤过敏一碰就红——情绪版的"过敏体质"就是 irritability。

## 同义词
peevishness, testiness, petulance, temper
