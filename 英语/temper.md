# temper

## 音标
/ˈtɛmpər/

## 释义
n. 脾气，情绪状态——一个人短期的情绪表现，尤其是易怒程度（比 disposition 更"当下"，比 temperament 更短时效）
v. 使缓和，调剂（temper justice with mercy 宽严相济）

## 例句
He has a short temper in code review — mention his variable naming and you'll regret it for the rest of the sprint.

A good tech lead knows how to temper criticism with encouragement so juniors don't burn out.

## 记忆法
temper 和 temperament（气质）、temperature（温度）同源——都来自拉丁语 temperare "调节、混合"。脾气就像"情绪的温度计"：short temper = 温度计很短，一碰就爆表。
