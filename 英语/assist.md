# assist

## 音标
/əˈsɪst/

## 释义
v. 帮助，协助，辅助

## 例句
She offered to assist with the marketing of the product.

AI coding assistants assist developers by generating boilerplate code. *(自造句)*

## 记忆法

### 词根
> as- (ad-, toward 朝,向) + sist (站立) → 站到旁边 → 帮忙 → 帮助

> —
