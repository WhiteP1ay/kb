# concise

## 音标
/kənˈsaɪs/

## 释义
adj. 简洁的，简明的

## 例句
Please keep your answers concise — no more than three sentences.

A concise commit message tells what changed and why — not a novel about your afternoon.

## 记忆法
con-（一起）+ cise（切）= 把多余的一起切掉 → 简洁的。和 [[precise]] 同词根 cise（切）！

## 同义词
succinct, brief, terse
