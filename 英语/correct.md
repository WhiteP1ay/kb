# correct

## 音标
/kəˈrɛkt/

## 释义
adj. 正确的，对的；v. 纠正

## 例句
Is this the correct way to the station?

Your logic is correct but your implementation has a bug — the fix is just one line.

## 记忆法
cor-（一起）+ rect（直，同 erect 直立 / direct 指引）= 一起拉直 → 纠正，正确的。rect 词根意思是"直"，把弯的东西拉直就是纠正。

## 同义词
right, accurate, exact
