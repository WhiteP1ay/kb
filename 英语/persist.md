# persist

## 音标
/pərˈsɪst/

## 释义
v. 坚持；持续存在

## 例句
If the symptoms persist, consult your doctor.

The bug persisted even after three hotfixes. *(自造句)*

## 记忆法

### 词根
> per- (through, throughout 贯穿) + sist (站立) → 一直站着 → 坚持；持续

### 谐音
> —

## 同义词
continue, endure, persevere, linger
